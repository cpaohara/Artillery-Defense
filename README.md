# What is Artillery Defense?


## Overview
Artillery Defense is my submission for the my second module coursework of first year of university. We had to create an animated game using the HTML canvas and JavaScript.

This coursework scored 100% on the marking rubric, which included 5 sections:
- Animation
- Functional Coherence
- Interaction
- Code Structure
- Complextity

## How to play

The player controls a cannon located on the left side of the canvas, and must shoot and destroy incoming enemies. The goal is to get as many points as you can before being destroyed, you gain 1 point per enemy destroyed. There are 2 types of enemies: a car and tank. The car is generally the fastest enemy, and damages the player when it reaches the cannon. Whereas the tank is usually slower than the car, and can shoot projectiles at the player, which will damage them. The tank can also damage the player when they reach the cannon. Both enemies have varying speeds and hit points.

The player has 4 shells to choose from:
- Short range - Used for shooting enemies close to you
- Mid range - Used for medium distances or general use
- Long range - Used for shooting enemies far away
- Tracer - A special medium range shell that doesn't damage enemies, but will plot a dotted line where the projectile has been. Ideal for aiming

## Controls

- Barrel Pitch - [Up/Down] [W/S]
- Cycle Shells - [Left/Right] [Q/E]
- Fire Shell - [Space]
